Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be handled when you visit Balo Games, play a game, create or use an account, react to a game, or send feedback. Please do not submit passwords, payment details, government identification numbers, or other sensitive information through feedback forms.

1. Services used by Balo Games

Balo Games is published using GitHub Pages. The website also uses Supabase services for account authentication, game reactions and visitor-submitted messages. These providers process information as needed to provide their services and may process technical data such as IP address, browser details and service logs under their own terms and privacy policies.

2. Account and sign-in information

The website currently provides email-and-password sign-up/sign-in, Google sign-in and a guest option. When you use these options, Supabase Authentication processes account information and authentication identifiers. Google sign-in is also subject to Google's privacy terms. Balo Games does not receive your Google account password. Password authentication is handled by Supabase; do not share your password with us.

Guest use may create an anonymous authentication identifier so that game reactions or feedback can be associated with the same visitor session. If you create an account or sign in, authentication information may be associated with that account according to the provider's systems.

3. Game reactions and messages

When you use Like or Unlike, the website may send the game identifier, reaction type and visitor/account identifier to Supabase so reaction totals and your current reaction can be displayed. If you submit a game bug report or feedback message, the message and related visitor/account identifier may be stored in the Balo Games message service for review and site support.

The Contact form lets you optionally include a name and reply email address in your message. Only include a reply address if you want us to be able to respond. Do not include information that is not needed to explain your question or issue.

4. Browser storage

Balo Games uses browser storage for website features. For example, the Favorites feature stores your selected game titles in your browser's local storage. Supabase authentication may also use browser storage to maintain a sign-in session. You can clear website data through your browser settings; doing so may remove locally saved favorites or sign you out.

5. Cookies, analytics and advertising

The Balo Games website source currently reviewed for this policy does not contain a Google AdSense or analytics integration. If advertising, analytics or other tracking services are added later, this policy will be updated to identify the services and explain relevant choices. Third-party websites or content that you choose to open may use their own cookies or similar technologies under their own policies.

6. Games and external services

Games and features may load scripts, media or other resources. If a game or link connects to a third-party service, that service may receive technical information from your browser and its own privacy policy will apply. Balo Games does not control the privacy practices of third-party providers.

7. How information is used

Information described in this policy is used to operate sign-in, remember sessions, display reactions, receive bug reports and feedback, maintain website security, and respond to messages when a reply address has been provided. We do not describe these functions as a guarantee that every message will receive a reply.

8. Retention and requests

Information may remain with the relevant service for as long as needed to provide the feature, maintain records, address security issues or meet legal obligations. Actual deletion and retention are also subject to the settings and policies of the service provider. To ask about a message you submitted, use the Contact page and include enough detail to help us locate it. Do not send passwords or authentication codes.

9. Children's privacy

Balo Games does not ask visitors to provide sensitive personal information. Children should use online services with the guidance of a parent or guardian and should not submit personal contact details unless their parent or guardian has approved it.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy may change when website features or service providers change. The “Last updated” date above will be changed when we revise this page.

11. Contact

For a privacy question, use the Balo Games Contact page. The contact form sends a message through the website's message service. An optional reply email can be included in the message if you want a response.
