Cookie Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

This page explains the browser storage and similar technologies used by Balo Games. A browser may call some of these technologies “cookies” even when the website feature uses local storage or session storage instead.

1. Favorites

The Favorites feature stores selected game titles in your browser's local storage so your list can remain available when you return using the same browser. Clearing site data may remove these favorites.

2. Sign-in sessions

Supabase Authentication may store session information in your browser to keep you signed in, refresh a session and support guest access. This storage is used for account and visitor-session functionality.

3. Third-party services

Hosting, authentication, embedded resources, games or external websites may use their own cookies or similar technologies for security and service operation. Their practices are controlled by those providers and described in their own policies.

4. Analytics and advertising

The Balo Games source code reviewed for this policy does not currently include a Google AdSense or analytics integration. If such services are added, this page and the Privacy Policy should be updated to explain the technologies used and any available visitor choices.

5. Managing browser storage

You can review or clear cookies and site data through your browser settings. Blocking storage may affect favorites, sign-in sessions, reactions or other website features.

6. Updates and contact

This policy may change as website features change. For questions, use the Contact page.
